Ultima Forever FAQ

A guide to the world and systems of Ultima Forever: Quest for the Avatar, summarized from three archived forum posts by The Gypsy.

Historical note: The answers below describe the game as it existed in 2013. Ultima Forever’s online servers went offline on August 29, 2014, so the old download, login, account-transfer, and connection instructions can no longer restore access to the game. Read the end-of-service tribute for more context.

About the game

What is Ultima Forever?

The archived introduction describes a free-to-play multiplayer role-playing game for mobile, inspired by Ultima IV. Set in Britannia, it follows the Avatar’s effort to stop the Weep, a plague spreading from the Stygian Abyss. Players explored, completed quests and moral quandaries, collected equipment, and joined other players in dungeons.

What could players do?

The original FAQ highlighted character progression from levels 1 to 15, randomized loot, Tarot-card consumables, cosmetic outfits, Virtue and city-reputation progression, branching quests, puzzles, and cooperative dungeon runs for up to four players.

Gameplay

What did the different keys award?

  • Bronze: one Common or Uncommon item; Bronze Keys were found as dungeon loot.
  • Silver: one Uncommon or Rare item.
  • Gold: two Uncommon, Rare, or Epic items, with at least one Rare or better.

The archived post also described converting Bronze Keys to Silver and buying Silver or Gold Keys in the game store. Those were in-game options, not current services.

What did item rarity mean?

Common items were green, Uncommon blue, Rare purple, and Epic orange. Each tier offered stronger equipment, with Rare and Epic gear recommended for the toughest challenges.

What were Shrines for?

There were eight Shrines, one for each Virtue. Tending a Shrine temporarily boosted its Virtue; the original guidance said players could have all eight boosts at once and check their status on the map. Keys could be used to tend a Shrine without waiting for it to be ready.

What was the difference between Loadout and Clothing?

Loadout gear supplied the character’s stats. Clothing changed the character’s appearance, but wearing vanity gear did not replace the stats from the equipped Loadout.

Could a character be reset or changed?

The archived FAQ said a character could not be reset after its adventure began, and its class and base appearance could not be changed. Players could create both Mage and Fighter characters and change their visible outfit with vanity gear.

Could progress be continued on a new device?

At the time, the forum post advised signing in with the same Facebook account on the new device. It advised Guest players to link or upgrade their account to Facebook on the old device first. This depended on the live game service and is not a way to recover or transfer a save today.

Technical support

How did players install or update the game?

The 2013 instructions said to search for Ultima Forever: Quest for the Avatar in the device’s App Store and install available updates from its Updates tab. The post noted that a first launch could download Retina iPad graphics, localized assets (English, French, German, Russian, Japanese, or Korean), or small content updates. These are historical steps; the game’s servers are no longer online.

Why might the game have been patching?

According to the archived post, patches could install high-resolution iPad graphics, assets for a supported device language, or small developer updates. Changing the device language could trigger a different language’s assets.

What connection did the game recommend?

The original support answer recommended a strong, stable Wi-Fi connection for the best performance and said a carrier network might work with reduced performance. Regardless of connection quality, the now-closed servers prevent the game from connecting today.

What if the game would not install, load, or connect?

The archived advice included restarting the device, checking for an operating-system update, and using a stable network. Those steps only applied while the game was supported; they cannot bring the service back or fix a current login failure.
